## Research Interests

**Reinforcement Learning · Embodied AI · LLM Reasoning & Interpretability**

## Selected Research

### Trajectory-Based Credit Assignment

*Manuscript under review at NeurIPS 2026*

I study long-horizon credit assignment in cooperative multi-agent systems. I proposed a trajectory-based method that aggregates delayed feedback along entity trajectories for multi-intersection traffic signal control.

### Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning for Game AI

I developed a goal-conditioned hierarchical RL framework that decomposes long-horizon decision making into high-level strategy and low-level control, together with reward shaping, curriculum learning, and self-play.

*Hierarchical RL · Long-Horizon Decision Making · Self-Play · Multi-Agent RL*

### Generative Model Alignment

I worked on a speech generation pipeline combining an autoregressive Transformer with Flow Matching, and used reinforcement learning with a learned evaluator to improve generation quality.

*Generative Models · Transformer · Flow Matching · Reinforcement Learning · Alignment*

### Sim-to-Real Drone Navigation

I integrated NeRF-based scene reconstruction with physics simulation and trained closed-loop visual control policies using reinforcement learning and expert demonstrations, with transfer to physical quadrotor experiments.

*Embodied AI · Vision-based Control · Imitation Learning · Reinforcement Learning · Sim-to-Real*
