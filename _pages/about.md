I am a machine learning engineer with research experience in reinforcement learning, hierarchical decision making, sim-to-real visual control, and generative modeling. I received my M.Eng. in Computer Technology and B.Eng. in Traffic Engineering from Sun Yat-sen University, and worked at Parametrix.ai from 2022 to 2026.

My current research interests include **reinforcement learning**, **sequential decision making**, **embodied intelligence / vision-language-action models**, and **reasoning in foundation models**. I am particularly interested in long-horizon decision making, credit assignment, learning from interaction, and the connection between reinforcement learning and general-purpose agents.

## Selected Research

### Trajectory-Based Credit Assignment for Multi-Intersection Traffic Signal Control

*Manuscript under review at NeurIPS 2026*

I formulated **Trajectory-based Credit Assignment (TCA)** for cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning in entity-flow scenarios. The method aggregates delayed feedback along entity trajectories to address the noise-myopia trade-off in credit assignment, and is designed to be plug-and-play without modifying the underlying policy or network architecture.

**Topics:** Multi-Agent RL · Credit Assignment · Sequential Decision Making

### Sim-to-Real Drone Navigation

I constructed neural simulation environments by integrating Instant-NGP reconstructions with physics-based engines, and trained closed-loop vision-based control policies using reinforcement learning and behavior cloning from expert demonstrations. The resulting policy achieved zero-shot transfer to physical quadrotor navigation experiments.

**Topics:** Embodied AI · Vision-based Control · Reinforcement Learning · Imitation Learning · Sim-to-Real

### Hierarchical Decision Making for MOBA Game AI

I developed a goal-conditioned hierarchical reinforcement learning framework that decomposed long-horizon gameplay into high-level macro-strategy and low-level control. I also designed reward shaping, curriculum learning, and self-play schemes to improve exploration and policy learning under partial observability.

**Topics:** Hierarchical RL · Long-Horizon Decision Making · Self-Play · Partial Observability

### Generative Model Alignment for Speech Generation

I worked on a generative speech pipeline combining an autoregressive Transformer for semantic modeling with Flow Matching for acoustic generation. I also developed a reinforcement-learning-based generative alignment framework that optimized sequence-level audio quality against a learned evaluator.

**Topics:** Generative Modeling · Reinforcement Learning · Alignment · Flow Matching
